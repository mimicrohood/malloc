06 / FREQUENTLY ASKED

Read before adoption.

IS MALLOC AN INVESTMENT PRODUCT?

No. malloc is a companion and interface layer. It helps users understand market events and preserve experiences; it does not promise returns or tell users what to buy.

DOES MY PET GET SICK WHEN MY PORTFOLIO FALLS?

Never. A red day changes the habitat weather, not the pet's health. Growth rewards learning, consistency, and responsible behavior.

CAN I SELL THE COMPANION?

The core identity is account-bound and cannot be transferred. Optional cosmetics and memory artifacts can exist separately.

DO I NEED STOCK TOKENS TO ADOPT?

No. Any eligible wallet can mint a base companion. Supported holdings simply create richer Portfolio DNA.

WHAT HAPPENS AFTER MINT?

Your Habitat unlocks. Daily weather, learning rituals, forecasts, and market memories begin shaping evolution.
